Gridding Instructions

Use Grid settings to adjust the core radius and image orientation. Choose Apply grid settings after changing parameters.

Review flagged items in the review queue. Drag cores to correct their positions, then choose Continue to export.

Marker cores are placed automatically when their surrounding row and column lattice can be inferred.
