Center and radius comparison

Uniform overlays rendered from the application's measured coordinates, before and after refinement. Both columns use exactly the same original preview pixels, viewport, core identities and drawing style. Neural masks are omitted so the circle boundary remains visible. These are engineering comparisons on three images, with no independent clinical ground truth.

On 25 controlled incomplete-mask fixtures, mean center error changed from 5.83 to 0.71 preview pixels; mean radius error changed from 4.55 to 0.40 pixels. This measures the controlled fixtures and does not estimate accuracy on real slides.
